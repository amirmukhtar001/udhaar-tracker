# Privacy Policy - Smart Udhaar

Effective date: 2026-04-20

## Who we are

Smart Udhaar is a personal loan tracking app.

Support contact: support@udhaartracker.app

## What data we collect

- Account data: email address used for OTP sign-in.
- App data: borrower names, loan amounts, notes, payment history, reminder date/time.
- Technical data: authentication/session metadata required by Supabase Auth.

## How data is used

- To create and secure your account.
- To store and sync your loan records.
- To send reminder notifications for reminders you set.
- To provide customer support when you contact us.

## Data storage and security

- Account and loan data are stored in Supabase.
- Access controls are applied so users can only access their own records.
- The app uses secure tokens for authenticated requests.

## Notifications

- Notifications are used only for loan reminders created by you.
- You can disable notifications from device settings.

## Data sharing

- We do not sell your personal data.
- Data is shared only with service providers required to operate the app (for example, Supabase for authentication and database hosting).

## Data retention and deletion

- Your data remains until you delete it in the app or request deletion via support email.
- You can request account/data deletion by emailing support@udhaartracker.app from your registered email.

## Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect product or legal changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.

## Public policy URL for stores

Host this file publicly and place that URL in Play Console and App Store Connect.
